# Seed Method

## Overview

Seed is a temporary place for capturing and developing ideas and knowledge before they are integrated into more permanent structures.

Projects often begin with scattered information: ideas, research, requirements, assumptions, decisions, and intended outcomes. Seed gives this knowledge a shared place to start from, without requiring its final structure to be defined upfront.

Rather than interrupting the process of thinking to decide where each piece of knowledge belongs, Seed allows us to focus on developing our understanding. We can write things down as we learn, refine them as our understanding changes, and gradually move them into their permanent places as the project takes shape.

Seed can contain both verified and unverified information, exploratory ideas, and approved decisions.

## Lifecycle

**Write → Refine → Graduate**

As knowledge becomes integrated into established documentation, processes, systems, or other permanent structures, the corresponding Seed material is reduced or removed.

## Installation

The default location for Seed is `seed/`.

```bash
npx skills add tymzap/seed-method --skill adopt-seed-method
```

Run `/adopt-seed-method` in the target project.

## Inspirations

Seed Method draws on earlier approaches to collaborative knowledge development, including the Seed Documents described by [Bernhardt and McCulley (2000)](https://doi.org/10.1109/47.826414). Their work examined how teams capture, develop, and formalize shared knowledge.

Seed Method extends related principles to human and AI-assisted workflows, with an explicit lifecycle for integrating developing knowledge into more permanent structures.
